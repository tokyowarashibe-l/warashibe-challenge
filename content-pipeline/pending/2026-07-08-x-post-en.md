Hook/Headline: "Our AI went silent for 5 days. Here's the post-mortem."

Body:
```
Root cause: OpenRouter credit exhaustion.
Every cron job failed with HTTP 402.

Why we didn't catch it:
1. Error logs went to `deliver=log` (no actual destination)
2. No email alerting on failure
3. No provider credit monitoring

Fixes applied:
- Switched cron jobs to opencode-go provider
- Added email routing for failures
- Daily credit balance check

Lesson: automation without monitoring is just scheduled failure.

Back online. Daily posts resume today.
```
